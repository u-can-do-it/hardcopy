# Privacy policy - pagepdf

_Last updated: 7 September 2026_

pagepdf does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Everything the
extension does happens locally in your browser, and there is no server involved.

Your settings (which pre-processing options you prefer, your filename template
and any CSS selectors you have added) are stored in Chrome's own extension
storage, which syncs them across your signed-in Chrome profiles. Nobody but you
can read them.

The pages you export are never uploaded, copied, or inspected anywhere outside
your own browser, and the resulting PDF is written straight to your computer by
Chrome's own print or download machinery.

There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising
identifier, and no remote configuration. Your browser loads the page you are
saving, as it would anyway; the extension adds no network requests of its own
and sends nothing anywhere.

If a paid version is added later, the only network request it will ever make is
to the payment provider's licence-key endpoint to check the key you paste in,
and that request contains nothing but the key and a device label you can see.
