Compared with Ctrl+P

Chrome already saves pages as PDFs. Then you open one.

The print dialog renders what the site's print stylesheet tells it to, on a page nobody prepared. That is where the cookie banner across the top, the blank image boxes and the half-cut paragraph come from. Hardcopy fixes the page first, then prints it.
