Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Hardcopy does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Everything the extension does happens locally in your browser, and there is no server on our side for it to talk to.

No analytics No telemetry No account No tracking

What is stored, and where

Your settings — which pre-processing options you prefer, your filename template and any CSS selectors you have added — are stored in Chrome's own extension storage, which syncs them across your signed-in Chrome profiles. Nobody but you can read them.

What happens to the pages you export

The pages you export are never uploaded, copied, or inspected anywhere outside your own browser, and the resulting PDF is written straight to your computer by Chrome's own print or download machinery.

What is not collected

There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier, and no remote configuration. Your browser loads the page you are saving, as it would anyway; the extension adds no network requests of its own and sends nothing anywhere.

The paid version

If a paid version is added, the only network request it will ever make is to the payment provider's licence-key endpoint, to check the key you paste in. That request contains nothing but the key and a device label you can see.

Permissions, and why each one exists

  • activeTab and scripting — run the page clean-up and the print call on the one tab you clicked the extension on. No host permissions are requested, so the extension has no access to any page until you click it.
  • storage — saves your own settings. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • debugger — used only for the screenshot and one-click PDF commands, on the tab you chose, attached for the length of that one export and detached immediately after. It never reads network traffic or cookies. Chrome shows a yellow “is debugging this browser” bar while it is attached.
  • downloads and offscreen — write the finished file to your Downloads folder.
